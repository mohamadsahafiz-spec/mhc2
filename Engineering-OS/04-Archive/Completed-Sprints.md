# Completed Sprints

# Purpose

This directory preserves the historical record of completed engineering sprints.

A sprint should be archived only after its objectives have been completed, verified, and accepted.

Completed sprint records provide traceability, accountability, and historical context for future engineering work.

---

# Objectives

This archive exists to:

- Preserve engineering history.
- Record completed work.
- Document major milestones.
- Track project evolution.
- Support future retrospectives.

---

# Sprint Record Standard

Every completed sprint should contain:

- Sprint Number
- Title
- Project
- Objective
- Scope
- Deliverables
- Outcome
- Version
- Date
- Related Documents

---

# Naming Convention

Recommended format:

Sprint-001.md

Sprint-002.md

Sprint-003.md

...

---

# Completion Criteria

A sprint should only be archived when:

- Objectives are completed.
- Deliverables are verified.
- Engineering review is finished.
- Version has been updated.
- Outstanding blockers are documented.

---

# Engineering Principle

Completed sprint records are historical artifacts.

They should not be modified except to correct factual errors.

Future work belongs in new sprints rather than altering completed records.
