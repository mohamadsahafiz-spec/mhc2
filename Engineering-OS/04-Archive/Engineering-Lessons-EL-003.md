# EL-003 — Free-First Infrastructure

## Status

Approved

## Summary

A technically strong managed service can still be the wrong engineering choice when it creates an unnecessary recurring cost or long-term dependency.

## Lesson

Before activating a paid-capable service:

1. Confirm the actual requirement.
2. Check existing infrastructure.
3. Evaluate free/no-billing alternatives.
4. Understand free limits and paid thresholds.
5. Understand whether usage accumulates over time.
6. Evaluate retention and deletion behaviour.
7. Evaluate migration/exit cost.
8. Obtain explicit Founder approval.

## Reusable Rule

**Free-first is not anti-paid. It is deliberate capital control.**
